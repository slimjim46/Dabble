DoodleScrap – About & Privacy

Last updated: 24 October 2026

What this is

DoodleScrap is an anonymous, real-time drawing game. There are no accounts, emails or passwords.

What we store

  • A random player id and the display name you choose.
  • The two-letter country code of your most recent visit (from the hosting platform; never a city or address).
  • Coarse activity: login time and last-seen time. Sessions older than 90 days are deleted.
  • Your drawings on the shared canvas, daily challenge entries and votes, and the results of finished games (mode, scores, countries).

What we don't do

We don't store or log your IP address, and we don't sell data or show ads. Note that our hosting and infrastructure providers (for example Supabase, Vercel and Cloudflare) may process technical data such as IP addresses in their own logs.

Reports and moderation

Drawings can be reported. Content that enough people report may be hidden, and an admin may remove content or ban players.

Bot protection

If enabled, your browser contacts Cloudflare (challenges.cloudflare.com) for a bot check before an anonymous account is created.
